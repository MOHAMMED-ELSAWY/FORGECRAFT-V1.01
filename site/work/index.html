Demo project

Restaurant Digital Platform

A digital menu with online ordering for a restaurant or café, plus the dashboard the staff use to run orders.

Problem
Printed menus go out of date, orders arrive by phone and chat, and staff lose track of what is new, being prepared, or ready.
Solution
Customers browse the menu on their phone, build an order and send it. The team sees each order on a dashboard and moves it through preparation to delivery.

What it includes

  • Menu with categories, search and Arabic and English
  • Cart, order notes, delivery, pickup or table orders
  • Order dashboard with live status and daily totals
  • Sold-out toggle for menu items
HTMLCSSJavaScriptProduction: Node.js + PostgreSQL

Demo project

Clinic Management System

A management system for a clinic: appointments, patients, daily dashboard and reports in one place.

Problem
Appointments live in notebooks and chat, patient history is scattered, and the owner cannot see how the clinic is doing without asking around.
Solution
One system for booking and tracking visits, keeping patient records, and seeing the day and the month at a glance.

What it includes

  • Dashboard with today's schedule and key numbers
  • Appointment booking with status tracking
  • Patient records with visit history
  • Revenue and service reports, CSV export
